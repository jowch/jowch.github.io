I'm a PhD student in Bioengineering at [UCLA](https://www.ucla.edu/), working
with [Gerard Wong](https://samueli.ucla.edu/people/gerard-wong/). My research
follows two threads.

**Innate immunity.** I study antimicrobial peptides and the innate immune
system, and how they shape autoimmunity and development.

**Bacteria meeting a surface.** I study what bacteria such as *Pseudomonas
aeruginosa* and *Vibrio cholerae* do in the first moments after they touch a
surface, before a biofilm forms.

Alongside that, I build open-source tools for scientists who use code but
aren't software engineers. Reactive notebooks, interactive figures and AI
agents can let a researcher take on analyses that used to be out of reach,
and the result should stay rigorous and reproducible. That's the thread
running through everything on the [projects](/projects/) page.

Before UCLA, I studied Computer Science and Biology at [Washington University
in St. Louis](https://wustl.edu/) (BS and MS).
